FEAST OF THE PRESENTATION OF THE LORD

Mary the Mother of Jesus lovingly and willingly united herself to her Son’s sacrifice on the Cross, sharing in his self-giving for our salvation and redemption.  That is why several Popes have called Mary Co-Redemptrix.  Mary teaches us to have faith.  If our faith is week, we should turn to Mary.  Jesus dying on the Cross bequeaths his mother to all mankind and especially to all his disciples.

Second Collection this Sunday for the LIFE charity.  Your donations help to provide funding that enables direct housing provision for up to 90 expectant women at any one time; a free and accessible helpline (via phone, text, email, or in person) with the option of emotional support through a counselling and skilled listening service; the availability of posted pregnancy tests; and the distribution of baby supplies. Life’s wrap around approach empowers all its clients to choose life for their baby. Last year over 22,000 women received assistance, which meant that they could face pregnancy or pregnancy loss with courage and dignity.  Thank you for your generosity.
