EIGHTEENTH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME

 

Jesus taught his disciples how to pray.  He was a man of prayer - he knew its value and he wanted his disciples to be people of prayer as well.  Prayer seeks to open us up to communication and communion with God.  It maintains and deepens our relationship with him so that his light and love can flow into us and out into the world in what we say and do.
