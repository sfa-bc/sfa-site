SIXTEENTH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME

The thirteenth chapter of  Matthew’s Gospel presents several parables on the Kingdom.  All of them emphasise the presence of the Kingdom of God in history - a presence which involves a process.  In the parable of the wheat and the darnel Jesus teaches us that that the enemy of souls is at work in the world, seeking to destroy the good seed sown by Christ.  It also teaches us how difficult it is to distinguish between those who are in the Kingdom and those who are not and that, therefore, we should not judge others, for God will give the definitive judgement in the end.

Cell of Our Lady of Walsingham and St Francis – This Cell meets at St Francis on the last Thursday of every month at 7pm to pray Vespers and Compline with Exposition of the Blessed Sacrament until Benediction at 8pm.  This is an opportunity to deepen your prayer life by participating in the prayer of the Church. During this year celebrating St Francis there will also be an opportunity at these meetings to venerate the Relic of the Seraphic Saint. All welcome.

 

This month’s meeting of the Cell – As part of the celebration of the year of St Francis. The Cell of Our Lady of Walsingham and St Francis will meet at 7pm in the Chapel on Thursday 30th July for Exposition and Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament, Evening Prayer, the Rosary with Meditations, Night Prayer and finishing with Benediction around 8pm. All Welcome.

Pilgrimage to St Francis of Assisi Kenilworth.

In this year of St Francis, on 25th July we are planning pilgrimages from St Mary’s Warwick, St Peter’s Leamington Spa and Our Lady, Lillington to St Francis’ in Kenilworth. You will be able to either join a group walking from one of the churches or join the walk en route. If this is too far there will be a group walking from Abbey Fields Kenilworth to St Francis. The pilgrimages will be completed with a mass at 17:30.

If you would like to take part please register your interest, including your chosen starting point, by emailing Richard at rstaveley118@gmail.com or calling 07535 933 786.
