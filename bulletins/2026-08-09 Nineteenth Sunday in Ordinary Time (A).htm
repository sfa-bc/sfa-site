NINETEENTH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME

In today’s Gospel Peter’s faith is tested when the going gets tough.  When everything is going swimmingly we do not realise how small our faith is.  We find out about our insufficiency and our absolute dependency on Christ when we are buffeted by the storms of life.  As Thomas Merton says, when we have “nothing of our own to rely on, and nothing in our nature to support us, and nothing in the world to guide us or give us light - then we find out whether we live by faith.”  But it is when we know our poverty of spirit that we are blessed according to Jesus, for it is then that we are aware that we are not self-sufficient but that our sufficiency is all in him.

Relic of St Francis of Assisi – During this year of St Francis a Relic of St Francis will be available for veneration at St Francis Baddesley Clinton after the Masses on Saturday 6.30pm and Sunday 11.00.

 

Cell of Our Lady of Walsingham and St Francis – This Cell meets at St Francis on the last Thursday of every month at 7pm to pray Vespers and Compline with Exposition of the Blessed Sacrament until Benediction at 8pm.  This is an opportunity to deepen your prayer life by participating in the prayer of the Church. During this year celebrating St Francis there will also be an opportunity at these meetings to venerate the Relic of the Seraphic Saint. All welcome.

 

Celebrations for the Year of St Francis – Patronal Mass on Sunday 4th October. Cell of Our Lady of Walsingham and St Francis meeting monthly. Relic of St Francis available for veneration at the weekend Masses. Social events are being arranged. A talk and a video on St Francis from Mgr. Danny McHugh. Joining in pilgrimages to other Parishes who are also celebrating the year of St Francis. Loaning of the relic of St Francis to other Parishes. Also, there have been requests for other Parishes to make a pilgrimage to our Church.

 

Discussions and reflections of the Faith – I plan to run a course on one evening in the week at Blessed Robert Grissold starting around the end of September. If anyone is interested in exploring the Catholic faith especially with a view to being received into full communion, this course would be helpful. All welcome.

 

Pilgrimage to St Francis – We have a pilgrimage planned from OLMC Redditch to St Francis on Saturday 12th September in the afternoon. If you are willing to volunteer please give your names to Fr Frank.
