Solemnity of The Assumption of The Blessed Virgin Mary

The saints are our models of Christian life.  This is especially true of the Blessed Virgin Mary because of the place she occupies in the history of salvation.  Mary, our Lady of the Assumption, is the perfect Christian and as such she is the model for the whole Church.  She can be seen as the Church in miniature.  She shows us that we must be dedicated completely to Christ as she was, that we must be willing to suffer with him, as she did.  And she also shows us that loyalty to Christ guarantees immortality, body as well as soul in complete happiness in heaven.

Relic of St Francis of Assisi – During this year of St Francis a Relic of St Francis will be available for veneration at St Francis Baddesley Clinton after the Masses on Saturday 6.30pm and Sunday 11.00.

 

Cell of Our Lady of Walsingham and St Francis – This Cell meets at St Francis on the last Thursday of every month at 7pm to pray Vespers and Compline with Exposition of the Blessed Sacrament until Benediction at 8pm.  This is an opportunity to deepen your prayer life by participating in the prayer of the Church. During this year celebrating St Francis there will also be an opportunity at these meetings to venerate the Relic of the Seraphic Saint. All welcome.

Joining in pilgrimages to other Parishes who are also celebrating the year of St Francis. Loaning of the relic of St Francis to other Parishes.
