TWENTY-FOURTH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME

At the heart of the revelation of God in the scriptures is his gratuitous love and today’s Gospel

teaches us that this kind of love is to be the norm of our relationship with others. According to

Jesus’ teaching forgiveness is a vital part of the daily life of the Christian community. Peter

wants to know just how far our forgiveness should go. Jesus answers that we must always

forgive - that is the meaning of the mysterious expression “seventy-seven times.” This symbolic

number indicates that there is to be no limit to forgiveness in the Christian community for love is

not compatible with obligations that can be counted, rather it is gratuitous, like the love of God.
